# WORKFORCE INTELLIGENCE EXECUTIVE SUMMARY REPORT
**Focus:** Retention Risk & Cohort Stability Analysis  
**Reporting Date:** May 2026 (AI-Assisted Operations Support)

---

### 1. Executive Retention Summary
The organization maintains a current active headcount of **12,450 employees**. Predictive risk indicators have flagged **218 team members** as holding a **High Attrition Risk** status, representing **1.8%** of the total workforce. Operationally, the average tenure for active resources remains at **28.4 months**, and the annual voluntary attrition rate sits at **14.2%**.

### 2. Attrition Risk Hotspots
A multi-dimensional cohort analysis indicates that exits are heavily concentrated in three key clusters:
* **The Critical 1-2 Year Tenure Band:** Accounts for **68%** of all voluntary departures, indicating a need to re-evaluate onboarding-to-integration handoffs.
* **Functional Roles:** `Engineering (Software Engineers)` and `Customer Success & Support` exhibit elevated hazard ratios, strongly correlated with a high manager span of control (averaging `14.5` direct reports).
* **Location Deviations:** `Bengaluru Office` logged a **22.0%** increase in exits compared to the national average, primarily driven by competitive salary discrepancies.

### 3. Weighted Risk Factors Analysis
The weighted risk-scoring engine evaluated 14 features across the active population. The top contributing vectors to high risk tiers were:
1. **Low Engagement Scores (Weight 35%):** Present in 82% of high-risk files.
2. **Compensation Stagnation (Weight 25%):** Employees falling below the 30th percentile in their respective grade brackets.
3. **Promotion Stagnation (Weight 15%):** Job level lag exceeding 24 months without job grade adjustments.

### 4. Strategic Retention Recommendations
1. **Targeted Comp Adjustments:** Initiate compensation reviews for the `Engineering (Software Engineers)` cohort positioned below the 30th percentile to mitigate immediate market-churn risks.
2. **Manager Span Reductions:** Introduce team subdivisions in the `Customer Success & Support` department where manager span of control exceeds 12 direct reports.
3. **Engagement Interventions:** Direct HR Business Partners to conduct proactive interviews with the 218 employees in the High Risk category.
